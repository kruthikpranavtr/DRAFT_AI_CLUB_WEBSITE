01ABOUT THE CLUB

Where curiosity about intelligence becomes working systems.

The AI Club is the official student community of Sri Shakthi Institute of Engineering and Technology dedicated to artificial intelligence and machine learning. It exists so students can move beyond coursework — into projects, competitions and hands-on building.

Members learn together through study circles and workshops, represent the institution at hackathons, collaborate across departments, and share what they build with the wider campus. (Editable draft copy — official club description to be supplied.)
